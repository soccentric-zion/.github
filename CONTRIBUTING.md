# Contributing

Thanks for helping. These projects are MIT licensed and aimed at small teams
building embedded Linux devices.

1. Open an issue first for anything larger than a small fix.
2. Fork the repository and branch from `develop`.
3. Open your pull request against `develop`.
4. Sign off every commit (`git commit -s`). The sign-off certifies the
   Developer Certificate of Origin (https://developercertificate.org/): you
   wrote the change or have the right to submit it under the MIT License.
5. Keep changes focused, and include tests or build steps where they apply.

When you report a bug, tell us the board, image version, and relevant logs.
